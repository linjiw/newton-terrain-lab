Read the physics assumptions ↗

Demonstrated

  • Native two- and four-environment execution
  • Selective resets preserve peer states
  • Continuous surfaces from measured MPM samples
  • A finite, successful PPO optimizer update

Still to establish

  • Sand and mud calibration against real measurements
  • Long-horizon tracking and learning gains
  • High-throughput training at large batch sizes
  • General adapters beyond the tested SONIC fork

Water is excluded. Smooth surfaces improve the display, not the physics model. Importing USD or XML alone does not activate deformable materials.
